What to have ready for an AC repair visit

A useful appointment starts with a few simple details. You don’t need to take anything apart or make a repair before the technician arrives. Gather what you can from this short list:

  • The AC symptoms, including sounds, changes in cooling, or trouble starting.
  • When the problem began and whether it has changed since then.
  • Any steps you’ve already tried, such as adjusting the thermostat.
  • A clear path to the equipment and the thermostat, if possible.

A technician can assess the system and discuss the findings with you. Avoid guessing at a repair based on a brief online search or repeatedly restarting equipment that seems to be malfunctioning. If you smell something unusual, see a concerning leak, or feel unsafe around the equipment, stop using it and call for professional guidance.
