What to have ready before the visit

A little preparation can make the conversation and service visit more useful. Before calling or while you wait, consider this short checklist:

  • Note when you first noticed the trouble and whether it has changed.
  • Identify which rooms or parts of the home are affected.
  • Write down any display message or change you’ve observed, without opening the equipment.
  • Make a note of recent adjustments to the thermostat or heating schedule.
  • Ensure the technician can reach the equipment, and keep pets or household items clear of the work area.

Avoid repeatedly restarting equipment or attempting repairs that involve wiring or internal components. Those steps may not address the cause and can create additional risk. A professional can inspect the system and explain what the findings mean before discussing repair options.
